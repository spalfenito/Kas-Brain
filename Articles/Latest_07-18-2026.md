## 1. Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K3 — World's Largest Open-Source Model Ever

**Source:** [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/chinas-moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-the-largest-open-source-model-ever-rivaling-top-u-s-systems)

China's Moonshot AI released Kimi K3, a 2.8-trillion-parameter open-weight model and the first in the "3-trillion-parameter class" ever made publicly available. Built on a Mixture-of-Experts architecture that activates 16 of 896 experts per token, Kimi K3 features a 1-million-token context window and native visual understanding. In blind developer testing on Arena.ai's Frontend Code Arena, it ranked #1 with a 76% pairwise win rate — beating Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol. The full model weights are set for release by July 27, 2026, under a Modified MIT license.

## 2. 29 Nations Sign Founding Agreement for World AI Cooperation Organization (WAICO)

**Source:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/17/chinas-xi-jinping-launches-new-ai-alliance-what-is-it)

At the World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) in Shanghai, 29 countries — including Russia, Brazil, Indonesia, Pakistan, Cuba, Venezuela, Belarus, Kazakhstan, and Laos — signed the founding agreement for WAICO, an independent intergovernmental organization headquartered in Shanghai. Chinese President Xi Jinping called on nations to embrace the "historic opportunity" of open-source AI and ensure no single country dominates the technology. UN Secretary-General António Guterres attended the ceremony. WAICO creates a second institutional AI governance framework to rival the G7/EU-aligned bloc, marking a formal geopolitical split in global AI governance.

## 5. TSMC Posts Record $22B Quarterly Profit on Surging AI Chip Demand

**Source:** [AI to ROI](https://ai2roi.substack.com/p/ai-to-roi-news-and-analysis-july-103)

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) reported Q2 2026 net profit of approximately $22 billion — up 77% year-over-year — on revenue of $40.2 billion, a 34% increase. The record results were driven almost entirely by insatiable demand for AI accelerator chips from customers including NVIDIA, Apple, and AMD. TSMC's performance underscores that AI infrastructure spending remains robust despite concerns about a potential slowdown, and reinforces the chipmaker's position as the critical bottleneck — and biggest beneficiary — of the global AI buildout.
