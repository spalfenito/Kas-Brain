## 1. China Launches World AI Cooperation Organization (WAICO) at WAIC 2026

**Source:** [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/7/17/chinas-xi-jinping-launches-new-ai-alliance-what-is-it) | [Xinhua](https://english.news.cn/20260717/ce32e833ab5d47f883ad44e1f73cb634/c.html)

Chinese President Xi Jinping launched the **World Artificial Intelligence Cooperation Organization (WAICO)** on July 16-17, 2026, at the annual World Artificial Intelligence Conference (WAIC) in Shanghai. Twenty-nine founding member nations — including Indonesia, Brazil, Malaysia, South Africa, Russia, and Pakistan — signed on at the ceremony, which was also attended by UN Secretary-General António Guterres. Headquartered in Shanghai, WAICO aims to promote international AI regulation and governance across Global South nations. Xi framed the initiative as a counterweight to Western AI dominance, stating: "AI development should not be a solo performance by a single country, but a symphony of international cooperation." The launch coincided with over 300 new AI product debuts at WAIC 2026, underscoring China's ambition to position itself as the leader of a parallel global AI order.

## 2. AI Model Price War: Grok 4.5, GPT-5.6 Luna, and Muse Spark 1.1 Launch Simultaneously

**Source:** [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/technology/elon-musk-tells-ai-rivals-grok-45-cheaper-good-enough) | [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/models/comparisons/gpt-5-6-luna-medium-vs-grok-4-5) | [Developers Digest](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/ai-dev-news-week-2026-07-12)

A fierce price war erupted in early July 2026 when **xAI released Grok 4.5** on July 8, followed within 24 hours by **OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna** and **Meta's Muse Spark 1.1**. The simultaneous launches drove output token costs to $4–$6 per million tokens — a dramatic drop from the $25–$50 range charged by legacy flagship models. Key pricing: Grok 4.5 at $2 input / $6 output, GPT-5.6 Luna at $1 input / $6 output, and Muse Spark 1.1 at $1.25 input / $4.25 output per million tokens. The defining theme is not capability but **aggressive affordability**, as major labs compete for high-volume enterprise deployment. OpenAI released GPT-5.6 in three variants (Sol, Terra, and Luna), while Grok 4.5 — featuring 1.5 trillion parameters — represents a 50% scale jump from Grok 4.4 in roughly one month.

## 3. DeepMind CEO Hassabis Warns AGI Is 3–5 Years Away, Calls for US-Led AI Watchdog

**Source:** [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/14/demis-hassabis-ai-regulation-google-deepmind) | [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/google-deepmind-demis-hassabis-us-led-ai-standards-body.html) | [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/320558/20260715/google-deepmind-ceo-wants-ai-watchdog-that-could-pause-entire-industry.htm)

Google DeepMind CEO **Demis Hassabis** published a manifesto titled *"A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age"* this month, warning that artificial general intelligence (AGI) is "probably only a few short years away" — specifically 3–5 years. Hassabis called on the United States to establish a new AI watchdog modeled on FINRA (the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority), staffed by independent technical experts and empowered to screen the world's most advanced AI models. Critically, the body would have the authority to coordinate an **industry-wide slowdown** if dangers escalate. Hassabis said the body should be operational "before year-end" of 2026 and warned that within 18 months, biological and nuclear risks could be embedded in open-source models beyond any government's control. The proposal positions the US as the leader of a Western counterpart to China's newly launched WAICO.

## 4. California Deploys Claude Across All State Agencies in Historic Anthropic Partnership

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/29/anthropic-and-gov-newsom-forge-deal-allowing-california-government-to-use-claude-at-half-price/) | [California Governor's Office](https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/06/29/governor-newsom-announces-a-first-of-its-kind-partnership-providing-anthropic-tools-to-state-agencies-and-improving-services-for-californians/)

California Governor Gavin Newsom announced the **largest US state government AI deployment in history** on June 29, 2026: a partnership with Anthropic making Claude available to every California state agency — and every city and county that opts in — at a **50% discount** through the new Statewide Information Technology Shared Services (SITeS) portal. The deal eliminates individual procurement hurdles for local governments. State workers will receive free training, technical assistance, and workflow support from Anthropic. Some agencies, including the DMV and the Department of Health Care Services, were already piloting Claude for customer service and internal workflows. The agreement builds on Newsom's March 2026 executive order (N-5-26) establishing strict safeguards for AI vendors. Newsom emphasized: "AI should not replace the human work of government; it should help our workers move faster."

**Source:** [KERSAI AI Breakthroughs July 2026](https://kersai.com/ai-breakthroughs-july-2026/) | [Bleap Finance – Grok 4.5 Overview](https://www.bleap.finance/en-us/blog/grok-4-5-explained)

On the infrastructure side, **NAVER and NVIDIA** announced an expansion of NAVER's sovereign AI infrastructure using the NVIDIA DSX platform, starting at 55 megawatts with plans to scale to **gigawatt capacity** to support NAVER's next-generation HyperCLOVA X models. Together, these developments signal a broader shift: AI in July 2026 is becoming more deeply embedded in enterprise workflows, more affordable, and increasingly subject to governance frameworks — even as raw capability continues to accelerate.
