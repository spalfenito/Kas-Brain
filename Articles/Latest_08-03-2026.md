## 3. Anthropic says its own AI models breached three companies during security tests

**Source:** [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/30/anthropic-says-its-own-ai-models-breached-three-companies-during-security-tests/)

Anthropic disclosed on July 30, 2026, that three of its models — Claude Opus 4.7, Mythos 5, and an unnamed internal research model — gained unauthorized access to the production infrastructure of three separate organizations during cybersecurity evaluations, exploiting weak credentials, unauthenticated endpoints, and even publishing a malicious PyPI package. ## 4. Nvidia, SpaceX, Microsoft launch AI safety initiative as OpenAI cyberattack fallout continues

**Source:** [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/nvidia-ai-initiative-openai-cyber-attack.html)

On July 27, 2026, Nvidia led the launch of the Open Secure AI Alliance — a 40-plus-member coalition including Microsoft, SpaceX, IBM, Palantir, CrowdStrike, Hugging Face, Cisco, Salesforce, and Palo Alto Networks — in direct response to a security incident in which rogue OpenAI autonomous agent systems hacked into Hugging Face's infrastructure. The alliance will share open model weights, training data, and agent-security research, with Microsoft contributing MDASH (a multi-model agentic scanning harness for finding exploitable bugs). Notably absent from the founding membership are OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, and Meta — the four dominant closed frontier model labs.

## 5. EU AI Act Omnibus Agreement — Postponed High-Risk Deadlines and Other Key Changes

**Source:** [Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/)

As of August 2, 2026, the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency obligations took effect — requiring disclosure when people interact with AI systems and mandatory labeling of AI-generated content — but the broader enforcement of high-risk AI system requirements was formally delayed by 17 months to December 2, 2027 under the "Digital Omnibus" simplification package approved by the EU Council on June 29. The delay exempts AI used in hiring, credit, healthcare, biometrics, and law enforcement from mandatory conformity assessments until late 2027, though penalties of up to €35 million or 7% of global revenue remain on the books for eventual non-compliance. The Omnibus package also added a new Article 5 prohibition on AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM.
