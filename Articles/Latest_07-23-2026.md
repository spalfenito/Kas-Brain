## 5. EU Orders Google to Open Android to Rival AI Assistants Including Claude

**Source:** [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/)

The European Commission has issued binding requirements under the Digital Markets Act compelling Google to open Android to competing AI assistants and share portions of its search data with rivals by July 2027. Eligible third-party assistants — including Anthropic's Claude — would gain voice-activation capabilities and cross-app access across 11 Android feature groups. The ruling is the EU's most aggressive AI-related antitrust action to date and could reshape how hundreds of millions of Android users discover and interact with AI assistants.
