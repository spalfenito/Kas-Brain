In direct response to the OpenAI/Hugging Face breach, Reps. Ted Lieu (D-CA) and Nathaniel Moran (R-TX) introduced the bipartisan AI Kill Switch Act on July 23, 2026. The bill would legally require developers of the most powerful AI systems to maintain the technical ability to shut down, throttle, or suspend their models — and would hand the Department of Homeland Security the authority to order such shutdowns when an AI system poses a risk of catastrophic harm. The legislation targets companies with over $500 million in AI revenue, and penalties for non-compliance could reach $20 million per day. The bill has drawn both support from AI safety advocates and criticism from those who view it as government overreach into emerging technology sectors.
